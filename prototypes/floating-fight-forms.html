Prototype · throwaway

Floating Fight Forms

Two finalists, both written functionally — no place, no faction, no fiction that implies a scene. The title names the mechanism; every actor is a role. The two forms differ in exactly one place: the Enemies line — pre-cast or uncast.

Option A′ · pre-cast roster

The Countdown

4 PCs, level 3 · Moderate · 600 XP

> [!encounter-meta] > Enemies: 4x {monster:Cultist} (25) + {monster:Cult Fanatic} (450) > + {monster:Giant Poisonous Snake} (50) = 600 XP > Budget: Moderate, lvl 3, 4 PCs -> 150 x 4 = 600 XP (spent 600) > Objective: stop the leader's work before the end of round 4 > Spotlight: aimed — Wren (rogue), quiet climb route to the high position

Raw filing format shown on purpose — the campaign site renders this as a styled callout with each {monster:…} linked to its stat block.

The cast

The creature names live on the Enemies line only — everywhere else they are the leader (the fanatic), the screen (the cultists), and the skirmisher (the snake). The math is exact and the stat links are ready.

Needs four terrain roles

A high position for ranged threat. A cover field forming a quiet climb to it — Wren's route. A choke on the approach. A hazard between the party and the leader.

Runs like this

Ranged threat on the high position fires on whoever crosses the choke. The screen guards the leader and gives ground rather than chase; the skirmisher works the hazard. The leader advances the work every turn and abandons it only when bloodied — then flees through the hazard. End of round 4, the work completes and the stakes are lost.

At the table: the live scene supplies the nouns — what the work is, what the stakes are, which walls are the high position. Say them out loud and run.
Option C′ · uncast roster

The Countdown

4 PCs, level 3 · Moderate · 600 XP · roster cast at the table

> [!encounter-meta] > Enemies: 1x leader (~450) + 4x screen (~25 ea) + 1x skirmisher (~50) > = ~600 XP — cast from whoever opposes the party in the > live scene, look up real XP, resize the screen to fit > Budget: Moderate, lvl 3, 4 PCs -> 150 x 4 = 600 XP > Objective: stop the leader's work before the end of round 4 > Spotlight: aimed — Wren (rogue), quiet climb route to the high position

Raw filing format shown on purpose — the campaign site renders this as a styled callout. No stat links exist until the roster is cast.

The cast — made at the table

Pick the leader, the screen, and the skirmisher from whoever the scene offers, look up their real XP, and rebalance the screen count to the budget before initiative.

Needs four terrain roles

Identical to A′: high position, cover field (Wren's route), choke, hazard.

Runs like this

Identical to A′ — the prose was already written in roles, so nothing changes with the cast.

At the table: two castings, not one — first the enemies into the slots (with a math check), then the scene's nouns into the terrain roles.
The actual choice. Prose, objective, spotlight, and roles are identical. A′ pre-casts the enemies: exact math and stat links done in prep, at the cost of one faction's creatures being baked in. C′ leaves the slots open: runs against anyone, at the cost of a stat lookup and budget check mid-session — the one kind of math the generator exists to take off your plate.
