# Maren Tallow

Mayor of [Emberwick](../locations/emberwick.md) and master of its
chandlers' guild — the two offices have been one chair for eighty years.
Sixty, broad-shouldered, wax burns on both forearms; talks like a ledger,
pays like one too.

- **Wants:** the lantern failures explained and stopped before the autumn
  caravan season, when buyers will test every lantern on the dock.
- **Fears:** that the cause is the guild's own history — the Cinder Court
  expulsion was her mother's doing, and the records of it are sealed in
  the guildhall basement.
- **Leverage:** controls the posted reward, the guild's favor economy, and
  access to the sealed records. Will trade the last of these only when
  cornered by evidence.

## Favors owed

- To the party, after session one: passage on any guild caravan in the
  Vale, once. (Promised at the reward payout; not yet called in.)
