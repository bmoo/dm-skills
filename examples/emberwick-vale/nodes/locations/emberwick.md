# Emberwick

A market town of some six hundred souls, built around the chandlery trade:
Emberwick tallow-lanterns burn for a year on one filling, and every caravan
on the Vale road buys them. The town's seal is a lit lantern; its motto,
"We Keep the Light."

Three weeks ago, lanterns across town began going out — not guttering,
*going out*, flame pinched as if between wet fingers. The chandlers'
[guild](/nodes/factions/chandlers-guild.md) is quietly frantic. Mayor [Maren Tallow](/nodes/npcs/maren-tallow.md)
has posted a reward for anyone who can find the cause.

## What the town knows

- The failures started in the warehouse quarter and are spreading uphill,
  toward the guildhall.
- Old-timers mutter about the [Cinder Court](/nodes/factions/cinder-court.md),
  the ash-cult the guild drove out a generation ago.
- A hedge-witch on Millrace Lane will say, for a coin, that the flames are
  not dying — they are *being collected*.

## On offer

- The mayor's posted reward: 200 gp for the cause, named and proven.
