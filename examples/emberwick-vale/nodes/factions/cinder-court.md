# The Cinder Court

An ember-venerating order that once shared the guildhall with the
chandlers: the guild kept the light, the Court kept the *ash*, and each
thought the other the junior partner. The expulsion — over a doctrinal
point the sealed records won't name — scattered the Court into the Vale's
hills a generation ago.

They are back, and they are not relighting old shrines. Court lay-brothers
are *collecting* Emberwick's flames — each pinched-out lantern is a wick
ritually reversed, its year of stored burning banked somewhere else.

- **Goal:** bank enough stolen flame to relight the Grand Cinder, the
  hearth the guild doused at the expulsion.
- **Method:** quiet, patient, inside jobs — they still have friends in the
  warehouse quarter, and one never-exiled lay-brother owned the Unlit
  Lantern warehouse outright.
- **Weakness:** the collection must move uphill toward the guildhall in
  ritual order; the pattern of failures *is* their route, legible to
  anyone who maps it.

## Clue web

- Warehouse-quarter failures map to a route → reveals the guildhall
  basement as the destination.
- The never-exiled lay-brother's name in the guild ledgers → reveals the
  Court still has standing inside the guild.
- Ash-marks over the doors of spared buildings → reveals which households
  pay the Court quiet tribute.
