# The Chandlers' Guild

The guild of [Emberwick](/nodes/locations/emberwick.md), where tallow-lanterns
burn for a year on one filling. [Maren Tallow](/nodes/npcs/maren-tallow.md)
is its master and the town's mayor; the two offices have been one chair
for eighty years.

## Ties and favors

- The [Cinder Court](/nodes/factions/cinder-court.md) once shared the
  guildhall with the chandlers. The guild kept the light, and the Court
  kept the ash, until the guild expelled the Court a generation ago.
- The expulsion records are sealed in the guildhall basement; Maren
  controls access.
- The guild quietly offers lifetime lantern credit to anyone who keeps
  the lantern failures out of the broadsheets.
- Maren has promised the party passage on any guild caravan in the Vale,
  once; the favor has not yet been called in.
