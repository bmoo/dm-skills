# Approved Items

Items on this list may be placed silently during prep. Anything not
listed needs the DM's yes before it lands on a page.

- {item:Potion of Healing} — freely, in sensible quantities.
- {item:Lantern of Revealing} — one exists in the campaign; the guild
  vault holds it. May be *foreshadowed* silently, placed only by the DM.
- Emberwick tallow-lanterns (mundane, year-burning) — freely; they are
  the local trade good.
- Gold up to 100 gp per placement without asking.
