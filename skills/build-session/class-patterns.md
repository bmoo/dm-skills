# Class staging patterns

Staging patterns per 2024 class — what to put in a scene so a class's
statement fires. **Patterns, not inventory** — the authoritative flagged list
for an actual PC lives on their player page (see the data ladder in
[`spotlight.md`](spotlight.md)); subclass and spell picks sharpen these
entries, and a niche pick is begging for a scene it can win. Verify any rules
text you stage on via [`rules-sourcing.md`](rules-sourcing.md) — several
abilities changed name or shape since 2014.

Aim at the *actual party*: the profile's player half (observed style)
outranks everything below.

## Barbarian

**Combat.** Minions to cleave and big hits to shrug off; hazards and AoEs
quietly pay off **Danger Sense**; occasionally an enemy that makes
**Reckless Attack** a real choice.

**Out of combat.** Strength as theater — gates to hoist, doors to break.
**Primal Knowledge** (skill checks with Strength while raging) invites the
muscle-first solution instead of routing every social beat through the face.

## Bard

**Combat.** **Countercharm** only exists if enemies frighten and charm — fear
auras and enchanters are Bard food. **Bardic Inspiration** now fires
retroactively on a failed D20 Test: stage tense, visible failure moments.
Threats they can interrupt or finesse beat raw damage races.

**Out of combat.** Let them be the face — and let charm *work* (Suggestion
doesn't hand over the kingdom, but it does get them past the gate). Talking
their way out of a fight is a core Bard win — price it as a real victory,
not a skipped encounter.

## Cleric

**Combat.** A horde of Undead at least once — trivializing it with
**Turn Undead** plus **Sear Undead** is the feature, not a problem. Check the
subclass's **Channel Divinity** and stage its niche. With a healer in the
party, don't pull punches: the clutch Healing Word / Revivify moment exists
because a PC hit 0 — just don't manufacture downings at another player's
expense.

**Out of combat.** Their god is a lens: undead desecration, faith politics,
omens. **Divine Intervention** is now a guaranteed free casting — give it
stakes worthy of a miracle.

## Druid

**Combat.** Wild Shape is a creativity engine — stretch the stat block and
get to yes unless it breaks the game. Terrain control needs victims: enemies
blunder through camouflaged Spike Growth (invisible if they didn't see it
cast). Give **Wild Companion** scout jobs.

**Out of combat.** **Speak with Animals is always prepared** (2024 Druidic) —
keep a cast of squirrels, gulls, and street trees in your pocket. Animals and
plants aren't tiny humans: "What are enemies?"

## Fighter

**Combat.** Read the build — the class is a chassis: the archer wants a perch
and sight lines, the Battle Master melee traffic for **Riposte**, the
Eldritch Knight spell woven with steel. **Weapon Mastery** properties (Push,
Topple, Vex…) are scene levers — a ledge behind the enemy makes Push a story.
Give **Action Surge** a moment worth surging for.

**Out of combat.** **Tactical Mind** (Second Wind spent on a failed check) is
new and easy to never notice — hand the Fighter hard checks with real stakes.

## Monk

**Combat.** The poster child — almost everything needs setup. **Shoot them**:
no ranged enemies, no **Deflect Attacks** (absurd is fine when earned — the
ballista bolt). Verticality, gaps, and chases feed **Step of the Wind** and
**Acrobatic Movement**. **Stunning Strike** wants a target whose lost turn
visibly matters — not a legendary-resistance wall every time. **Slow Fall**
implies heights worth falling from.

**Out of combat.** Parkour as problem-solving; the fastest character carrying
the message no one else can deliver in time.

## Paladin

**Combat.** Something juicy to smite — a Fiend or Undead that deserves the
crit-smite memory (2024 **Divine Smite** is a Bonus Action spell: a
countering caster makes it a duel, sparingly — see the anti-tell rules).
**Aura of Protection** plus AoE-save fights lets the Paladin *feel* the party
surviving because of them. **Faithful Steed** — stage a battle where riding
in is an option, and let them mount up.

**Out of combat.** Oath as compass: temptations, judgment calls, protection
of the weak. **Lay on Hands** curing Poisoned makes poison plots personal.

## Ranger

**Combat.** The 2024 spine is **Hunter's Mark** — give them a target to
*hunt*: a fleeing quarry, a specific mark among many. Terrain spells deserve
victims. A Beast Master's companion wants tandem-strike moments.

**Out of combat.** Put exploration *on* them, by name, and pass the tracking
result as a note so the Ranger, not the DM, narrates the find. Foraging,
navigation, weather-reading: ask them first. Same animal and plant scenes as
the Druid where their spells support it.

## Rogue

**Combat.** **Sneak Attack every turn is the design target**, not an
exploit — provide the flanking ally, the shadows, the **Steady Aim** perch.
**Cunning Strike** trades dice for Poison/Trip/Withdraw — enemies whose
position or action economy matter make those trades delicious. The
disarm-mid-combat set piece: know whether your player loves the
side-objective or resents missing a round of stabbing.

**Out of combat.** Skill checks are home turf. **Reliable Talent** is the
"I can't roll below a 25" fantasy — let the DC-30 lock and the impossible
ceiling walk exist so only the Rogue can beat them.

## Sorcerer

**Combat.** **Metamagic** is the identity: Subtle wants a scene where casting
visibly is dangerous (bound, watched, at the gala); Careful wants allies in
the blast. **Innate Sorcery** sells when its round visibly flips an outcome.
Telegraph the enemy mage's showpiece on purpose and let **Counterspell**
answer it.

**Out of combat.** Their magic is *innate* — NPCs react to what they are;
weird magical phenomena resonate with their bloodline before anyone else
notices.

## Warlock

**Combat.** Pact Magic recharges on Short Rests — an adventure rhythm with
real short rests is itself a Warlock spotlight. Invocations are picks to
honor: **Devil's Sight** demands darkness (including their own), **Repelling
Blast** demands ledges, pits, and formations worth breaking.

**Out of combat.** The patron is a live NPC — **Contact Patron** is an
explicit invitation to answer. Whispered bargains, patron errands, the price
of power: give the pact screen time.

## Wizard

**Combat.** Let the encounter-ender end an encounter — the Fireball that
clears the goblins, the Wall of Force that splits the fight. Balance across
the *session*, not by nerfing the spell. The **Counterspell** duel is peak
Wizard — bait it with the enemy caster's visible trump card. Niche prepared
picks are statements: build at least one scene a chosen spell can win
outright.

**Out of combat.** They love feeling smart: lore to spout, mysteries to be
the detective on, arcane machinery only Arcana explains. **Memorize Spell**
pays off when the next problem is telegraphed — telegraph it. Familiars want
jobs.
