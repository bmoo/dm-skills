# Campaign canon record extract — Grayharbour, before session 3

A durable extract of what the record already holds on the subjects tonight
touches. Handed to the checker as its own named input, on the party roster's
precedent: the checker has no filesystem reach into the campaign record.

## Places

- **The Grayharbour Museum of Natural History** stands on Museum Square. Its
  public wings and its closing time are on the record; its after-hours security
  posture is not.
- **The Gilded Folio** is next door to the museum. **The university** is less
  than a mile off.
- **The Gloamfen** is a peat dig a week's travel out. A crew working it sold an
  unclassified find to the museum's annex.

## People

- **Dr. Isolde Fenwick** — a naturalist, dismissed from the university over an
  after-hours incident she says she did not cause.
- **Orla Selke** — the museum's curator, gracious in public.
- **Samira Romero** — captain of museum security.

## Revelations in progress
