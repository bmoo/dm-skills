## N1

The washhouse stair. The yard door above stands open all night, and the iron
gate at the bottom is locked — DC 15 with thieves' tools, or the washerwoman's
key. A dry well in the corner drops straight to the flooded gallery below;
anyone who wants the quiet way down takes it, DC 12 Athletics to climb.

> [!dm-sidebar]
> **Spotlight (scene):** Vex — exploration; the gate's lock is old enough to
> read, and the scoring on its plate says someone has picked it recently.

## N2

The ossuary. Behind the bone rack the mortar is newer than the bricks — DC 12
to spot, and the arch behind it opens into the gallery. The Sighted keep their
own dead here and do not care to be watched doing it.

> [!encounter-meta]
> **Party:** 5 PCs, Level 4
> **Enemies:** 3 × {monster:Ghoul} (2,700 XP) → **2,700 XP**
> **Budget:** moderate, level 4, 5 PCs = 500 × 5 = **2,500** (2,700 spent, −200)
> **Terrain:** the bone racks — heavy cover, and the standing water below the
> ossuary lip is difficult ground
> **Spotlight:** plain — fiction-first, nobody aimed at
> **Objective:** get out of the ossuary with the reliquary

## N3

The flooded gallery. The bricked arch back to the ossuary is obvious from this
side. The well shaft overhead is the way the party came, if they came that way.

## Conclusion

The night ends at the washhouse, one way or another.
