## Key Plot Points

- **The bell is a lure.** The smugglers ring it to run the blockade; the party can learn the signal and turn it.
- **The tower is haunted.** A bound wraith guards the bell and answers only to the old harbor-song.

## Adventure Background

The harbormaster has been selling the blockade's schedule to the coast-road
syndicate, and the bell tower is how the signal goes out.

> [!art-right]
> ![The harbormaster](marta.png)
> Marta counts the night's cut.

The party arrives the night the syndicate means to run its biggest cargo, and
the tower's bound wraith has begun to stir against the misuse of its bell.

## Beginning the Adventure

> [!read-aloud]
> The fog comes off the water thick as wool, and somewhere above you the great
> bell of Redwood Watch tolls once — wrong, off its hour.

Run the opening as the approach to the gatehouse, then hand off to `T1`.

**Features.** The whole watch is fog-wet stone; light is lantern-only; sound
carries, so the bell is heard everywhere.

### T1 — Gatehouse

The gatehouse guards the only landward way in.

> [!art]
> ![The gate gives](gate.png)
> The gate gives under the party's press.

The signal-song is chalked on the guard-room wall:

- **Show:** a rhythm of tally-marks scratched by the bell-pull, with a
  watch-change scrawled beside each cluster.
- **They learn:** the smugglers ring the bell on the blockade's watch rotation,
  and the next run is at the midnight change of watch tonight.
- **Points at** *(behind the screen):* the smugglers' timetable; the party can
  ring a false signal to scatter the run. It does not reveal who pays for the run.

> [!encounter-meta]
> **Party:** 3 PCs, Level 5
> **Enemies:** {monster:Bandit} × 4 (25 XP) → **100 XP**
> **Budget:** Low, level 5, 3 PCs = 500 × 3 = **1500** (100 spent, 1400 remainder)
> **Terrain:** the gatehouse — murder holes above the arch
> **Spotlight:** aimed at Vex — a smuggler shoves a crate off the walk to bait Vex's Sentinel reach
> **Objective:** breach the gate before the bell can ring

### T2 — The bell tower

The bell tower rises over the whole watch.

> [!dm-sidebar]
> **Spotlight (scene):** Sera — social; the harbormaster tests her against the
> old harbor-song in parley, and the tell is the ward-sigil inked on the ledger.

The ledger sits open on the bell-keeper's desk:

- **Show:** a harbor ledger whose margin tallies match a cut skimmed from every
  flagged cargo, initialed by the harbormaster.
- **They learn:** the harbormaster takes a share of every smuggled run.
- **Points at** *(behind the screen):* the harbormaster's office node; the party
  can confront or blackmail her. It does not name the syndicate above her.

> [!art]
> ![The bell falls](bell.png)
> The bell-wraith unbound as the great bell finally falls.

> [!encounter-meta]
> **Party:** 3 PCs, Level 5
> **Enemies:** {monster:Specter} × 1 (200 XP) → **200 XP**
> **Budget:** High, level 5, 3 PCs = 1100 × 3 = **3300** (200 spent, 3100 remainder)
> **Terrain:** the bell tower — the set piece, a swaying platform over the drop
> **Spotlight:** aimed at Bram — the wraith hauls an ally toward the drop and Bram's Grapple decides it
> **Objective:** put the bell-wraith to rest before the signal-hour

## Conclusion

The night resolves on whether the party rang the false signal, put the wraith to
rest, or turned the harbormaster. Reward: the harbor-song, worth a favor from the
tower hereafter.

- **Lead →** the ledger points to the harbormaster's office (node N-harbor).
- **Lead →** the signal timetable opens the coast road the syndicate runs (node N-coast).
- **Foreshadow:** a sealed letter in the tower bears the syndicate's mark, not yet actionable.
