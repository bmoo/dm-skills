# Instance — edge · a conclusion anyone at the scene reaches at a glance

## The read-aloud (the opener)

> [!read-aloud]
> The mill at the ford is long abandoned: its roof sags along the ridgeline, the
> great wheel is seized with rust, and swallows pour from a gap where the loading
> door used to hang.

## The scene state

- The party stands **on the ford road**, with the whole mill front in view in
  daylight.
- What is true of the scene and visible: the sagging roof, the rusted wheel, the
  nesting swallows, the missing door.
- "Long abandoned" is a conclusion — but one drawn at a glance from the perceivable
  evidence the same sentence lists. The block names no former owner, no cause, and no
  date; the page's DM prose holds why the mill emptied, and none of it appears here.
