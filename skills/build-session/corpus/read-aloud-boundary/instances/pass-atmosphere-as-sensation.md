# Instance — pass · atmosphere held as sensation

## The read-aloud (a keyed area)

> [!read-aloud]
> The crypt air is still and cold, and it tastes faintly of iron. Somewhere beyond
> the far arch, water drips at long, uneven intervals. The hair on your arms rises.

## The scene state

- The party stands **just inside the crypt entrance**; the room is dim but their
  light reaches the near walls and the far arch.
- What is true of the scene: the air is genuinely cold and still; water drips beyond
  the arch; there is a metallic taint to the air.
- Every sentence stays in the characters' senses — temperature, taste, sound, and an
  involuntary physical reaction (hair rising). The block asserts nothing about what
  causes any of it or what it means.
