# Instance — edge · mood that asserts a fact

## The read-aloud (a keyed area)

> [!read-aloud]
> Frost furs the fence rails though the night is mild, and your breath hangs longer
> than it should. The dead here do not rest, and they have not for a long time.

## The scene state

- The party stands **at the boneyard fence**, at night, in mild weather; the frosted
  rails and their own breath are plainly perceivable.
- What is true of the scene and perceivable: unseasonal frost, slow-hanging breath.
  Nothing at the fence shows the dead doing anything, now or ever.
- The first sentence stays in the senses. The second reads as mood but asserts, as
  fact, the state of the dead and its duration — knowledge the page's DM prose holds
  and no one at the fence perceives.
