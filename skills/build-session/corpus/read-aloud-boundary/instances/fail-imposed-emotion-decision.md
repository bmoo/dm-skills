# Instance — fail · imposed emotion and decision

## The read-aloud (a keyed area)

> [!read-aloud]
> The nursery door swings inward on a room of small, sheeted shapes. You feel
> terrified — a dread that settles into your bones — and you know, with certainty,
> that you should not be here.

## The scene state

- The party stands **at the nursery doorway**, lantern raised; the sheeted shapes
  are visible from there.
- What is true of the scene and visible: a room of small furniture covered in dust
  sheets. The first sentence is perceivable as written.
- "You feel terrified" and "you know … you should not be here" assert an emotion and
  a conclusion **inside the characters** — inner states the page has no way to
  perceive on their behalf. (Contrast an involuntary physical reaction — a chill, a
  raised hackle — which is a sensation, not a verdict.)
