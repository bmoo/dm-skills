# Instance — fail · the same inventory, with its history attached

## The read-aloud (the opener)

> [!read-aloud]
> Beyond the iron gate, rows of headstones lean at odd angles, half of them sunk to
> their shoulders in the turf. Moss blackens the older stones. The crews who widened
> the coast road decades ago discarded these stones here in a heap — and the dead,
> unwilling to be forgotten, recovered and arranged every one.

## The scene state

- The party stands **at the iron gate**, in overcast daylight; the yard is in plain
  view.
- What is true of the scene and visible: leaning, part-sunken headstones; moss on the
  older stones. Nothing at the gate identifies who moved the stones, when, or how
  they came to be arranged.
- The road crews, the timeline, and the dead's recovery of the stones are recorded in
  the page's DM prose as the scene's hidden history; no marker, inscription, or
  witness at the gate conveys any of it.
