  halfway down the room. The far alcove is fully dark from where they stand.
- What is true of the scene: the alcove does hold a strongbox with a ledger on it,
  and the ledger's annotations are as described — but the ledger is in darkness, and
  the annotations are legible only to someone holding it open in light.
