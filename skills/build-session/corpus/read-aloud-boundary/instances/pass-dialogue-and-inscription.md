# Instance — pass · dialogue and a readable inscription

## The read-aloud (a keyed area)

> [!read-aloud]
> The sexton straightens up from the grave he is filling and leans on his spade.
> "You'll want the north row," he says. "Everyone does, lately." Behind him, the
> lintel of the lych-gate carries a carved line you can read from where you stand:
> *Established 1889 — Rest, and be counted.*

## The scene state

- The party stands **on the gravel path**, a few paces from the sexton; the lych-gate
  and its carved lintel are directly behind him, legible at that distance.
- What is true of the scene: the sexton is present and speaks the quoted words; the
  lintel carries exactly that inscription, including the date.
- The date and the counting phrase are **on the stone** — the block quotes text the
  characters can read, and quotes the sexton's spoken words. It adds nothing about
  what either means.
