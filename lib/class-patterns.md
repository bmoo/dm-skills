# Class staging patterns

Staging patterns per 2024 class — what to put in a fight so a class's
statement can fire. **Patterns, not inventory** — the authoritative flagged
list for an actual PC lives on their player page (see the data ladder in
[`spotlight-doctrine.md`](spotlight-doctrine.md#the-data-ladder)); subclass
and spell picks sharpen these entries, and a niche pick is begging for a
fight it can win. Verify any rules text you stage on via
[`rules-sourcing.md`](rules-sourcing.md) — several abilities changed name or
shape since 2014.

Aim at the *actual party*: the profile's player half (observed style)
outranks everything below. Every pattern stages an opportunity; the players
decide whether and how to take it.

- **Barbarian.** Minions to cleave and big hits to shrug off; hazards and
  AoEs quietly pay off **Danger Sense**; occasionally an enemy that makes
  **Reckless Attack** a real choice.
- **Bard.** **Countercharm** only exists if enemies frighten and charm — fear
  auras and enchanters are Bard food. **Bardic Inspiration** now fires
  retroactively on a failed D20 Test: stage tense, visible failure moments.
  Threats they can interrupt or finesse beat raw damage races; a fight talked
  out of existence is a core Bard win.
- **Cleric.** A horde of Undead at least once — trivializing it with
  **Turn Undead** plus **Sear Undead** is the feature, not a problem. Check
  the subclass's **Channel Divinity** and stage its niche. With a healer in
  the party, don't pull punches: the clutch Healing Word / Revivify moment
  exists because a PC hit 0 — just don't manufacture downings at another
  player's expense.
- **Druid.** Wild Shape is a creativity engine — stretch the stat block and
  get to yes unless it breaks the game. Terrain control needs victims:
  enemies blunder through camouflaged Spike Growth (invisible if they didn't
  see it cast). Give **Wild Companion** scout jobs.
- **Fighter.** Read the build — the class is a chassis: the archer wants a
  perch and sight lines, the Battle Master melee traffic for **Riposte**, the
  Eldritch Knight spell woven with steel. **Weapon Mastery** properties
  (Push, Topple, Vex…) are scene levers — a ledge behind the enemy makes Push
  a story. Give **Action Surge** a moment worth surging for.
- **Monk.** The poster child — almost everything needs setup. **Shoot
  them**: no ranged enemies, no **Deflect Attacks** (absurd is fine when
  earned — the ballista bolt). Verticality, gaps, and chases feed **Step of
  the Wind** and **Acrobatic Movement**. **Stunning Strike** wants a target
  whose lost turn visibly matters — not a legendary-resistance wall every
  time. **Slow Fall** implies heights worth falling from.
- **Paladin.** Something juicy to smite — a Fiend or Undead that deserves the
  crit-smite memory (2024 **Divine Smite** is a Bonus Action spell: a
  countering caster makes it a duel, sparingly — see the anti-tell rules).
  **Aura of Protection** plus AoE-save fights lets the Paladin *feel* the
  party surviving because of them. **Faithful Steed** — a battle where
  riding in is an option.
- **Ranger.** The 2024 spine is **Hunter's Mark** — give them a target to
  *hunt*: a fleeing quarry, a specific mark among many. Terrain spells
  deserve victims. A Beast Master's companion wants tandem-strike moments.
- **Rogue.** **Sneak Attack every turn is the design target**, not an
  exploit — provide the flanking ally, the shadows, the **Steady Aim**
  perch. **Cunning Strike** trades dice for Poison/Trip/Withdraw — enemies
  whose position or action economy matter make those trades delicious. The
  disarm-mid-combat set piece: know whether your player loves the
  side-objective or resents missing a round of stabbing.
- **Sorcerer.** **Metamagic** is the identity: Subtle wants a fight where
  casting visibly is dangerous (bound, watched, mid-parley); Careful wants
  allies in the blast. **Innate Sorcery** sells when its round visibly flips
  an outcome. Telegraph the enemy mage's showpiece on purpose and let
  **Counterspell** answer it.
- **Warlock.** Pact Magic recharges on Short Rests — an adventure rhythm
  with real short rests is itself a Warlock spotlight. Invocations are picks
  to honor: **Devil's Sight** demands darkness (including their own),
  **Repelling Blast** demands ledges, pits, and formations worth breaking.
- **Wizard.** Let the encounter-ender end an encounter — the Fireball that
  clears the goblins, the Wall of Force that splits the fight. Balance
  across the *session*, not by nerfing the spell. The **Counterspell** duel
  is peak Wizard — bait it with the enemy caster's visible trump card. Niche
  prepared picks are statements: stage at least one fight a chosen spell can
  win outright.
