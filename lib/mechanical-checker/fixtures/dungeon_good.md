# The Night Freight — keyed site

**Anchor:** Coalgate under-depot (node N-coalgate)
**Objective:** recover the forger's plates from the manifest vault (N7)
**Party:** 3 PCs, Level 5
**Scale:** one-session delve — 7 keyed areas, one level, 3 combats
**Signature technique:** Route loops — a service ring lets every approach rejoin
**Dungeon mechanic:** the freight timetable (not vanilla)

## The mechanic — the freight timetable

- **Trigger/Clock:** a train passes every 10 minutes of exploration, and an
  unscheduled engine is dispatched whenever an alarm pull is tripped.
- **Effect:** while a train runs, the through-tunnels (N5, N7) are lethal —
  crossing means a DC 15 Dexterity save against 4d10 bludgeoning; between
  trains they are open and the coupling gate unlocks.
- **Tells:** the rails humming underfoot, the chalked timetable board, signal
  lamps flipping from red to green down the line.
- **Exploit:** a party that reads the timetable crosses in the gaps and rides
  a flatcar straight into the vault before the next engine seals it.

## Keyed areas

- **N1 — Depot floor.** The obvious way in.
- **N2 — Signal gallery.** The alarm pulls live here.
- **N3 — Turntable hall.** The service ring's hub.
- **N4 — Coal chute.** The second approach, from above.
- **N5 — Through-tunnel.** The trains run here first.
- **N6 — Manifest antechamber.** Quiet, guarded.
- **N7 — Manifest vault.** The plates, and the set piece.
