# Redwood Watch

A fog-bound harbor watch built around a cracked bell tower, run by a harbormaster
who sells the blockade's schedule to the coast-road syndicate.

## The gatehouse

The landward way in, chalked with a smuggler's tally.

- **Show:** a rhythm of tally-marks scratched by the bell-pull, a watch-change beside each.
- **They learn:** the bell rings on the blockade's watch rotation; the next run is at the midnight change of watch.
- **Points at** *(behind the screen):* the smugglers' timetable; a false signal scatters the run.
