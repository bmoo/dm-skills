### Canon — what becomes true, and cannot be un-trued by editing

- **Premise.** The unclassifiable object the Gloamfen crew sold to the museum is
  an eldritch creature's egg that hatches tonight, and Dr. Fenwick asks the party
  to steal it out of the museum before it does, so she can neutralise it.
  *(rubric-graded: does the page enact it)*
- **Introduced canon.** Facts tonight asserts that the record does not already
  hold:
  - Eldritch eggs of this kind lie dormant for generations; unearthing one
    accelerates the hatch.
  - The hatchling is ravenous for raw meat and grows exponentially as it feeds.
  - The eggs are nearly indestructible, but encasing one in crystal neutralises
    it. Fenwick has built a crystal box for the purpose; it is not yet sealed.
  - The egg pulses from 10:30 p.m., and anyone near it makes a DC 10 Wisdom save
    or is affected.
- **Environmental ground rules.** The museum's after-hours state, stated before
  any room or NPC is keyed. Home: **introduced here**.
  - Interior doors are locked (DC 12 with thieves' tools); secret doors are DC 12
    to spot; ceilings are 30 feet.
  - Twelve guards hold fixed after-hours stations, and an alarm draws 1d4 more
    per round from the nearest station.
  - Alarm spells sit on the marked interior thresholds; staff pass cards bypass
    them; alarms are unarmed while the museum is open to the public.
- **NPC commitments.**
  - **Dr. Isolde Fenwick** — the party's client tonight; neutral good, genuinely
    trying to stop the hatch; **she survives the night.**
  - **Orla Selke** — the museum's curator; **neutral evil**, and secretly heads a
    syndicate fencing stolen objects.
  - **Samira Romero** — captain of museum security; stationed at the gala
    entrance after hours, and doing her job honestly.
- **Timeline commitments.** By the end of the night, in world-time: the gala has
  run 6–8 p.m.; the egg's effects have begun at 10:30 p.m.; **the egg hatches at
  midnight unless it is contained**; and the question of whether it is contained
  **resolves tonight, either way.**

### Web — what the clue web can no longer route around

- **Revelation paid down.** *The Gloamfen produced something that is not an
  artifact* — tonight moves it from 1 of 3 clues to **landed**: the party see the
  object themselves. *Unprovenanced finds are being papered* gains its third,
  independent clue.
- **Destination node(s).** [The Grayharbour Museum of Natural
  History](nodes/locations/grayharbour-museum.md), and specifically its **Gemstone
  Wing**, where the object is displayed.
- **Exit edge.** At least one progression lead out of the museum cluster, drawn
  from: a second egg still in the Gloamfen; the curator's syndicate as a thread
  the party can pull; or Fenwick's reinstatement at the university.

### Layout — what the geography commits to

- **Map topology.** The museum is a keyed interior on two floors plus a basement
  and an attic, with four entrances connecting it to Museum Square; the Gemstone
  Wing is the deep room and the guard stations sit between it and every entrance.
- **Fit to established geography.** *(rubric-graded)* The museum stands on Museum
  Square, the Gilded Folio next door, the university less than a mile off.
