# The museum after hours — keyed site

**Anchor:** the Grayharbour museum (node N-museum)
**Objective:** lift the sealed egg out of the Gemstone Wing (M7)

The Gemstone Wing is the deep room. Twelve guards hold fixed posts; the attic
and the café corridor are not among them, and the way down from the skylight is
the quiet way in.

## Keyed areas

- **M1 — Front hall.** Two on the desk, and the statues either side of it.
- **M2 — Rotunda.** The gala floor until eight.
- **M3 — Loading dock.** Below street level, out of sight of the square.
- **M4 — Undercroft.** Crates, and the stair up behind the panelling.
- **M5 — Upper landing.** Four guards, a brazier, the floor keys, the alarm pull.
- **M6 — Gemstone antechamber.** Two more, watching the wing's doors.
- **M7 — Gemstone Wing.** The egg, on the rigged pedestal.
- **M8 — Attic.** Nobody is stationed here and nobody comes up here.
- **M9 — Café corridor.** Dark after eight; the curator's office opens off it.
