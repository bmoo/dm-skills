# Node map — as-if: strike Rosalyn Briarwood's lead

Scope: the Lost Vein web at the end of Session 2. Struck: the one lead Rosalyn
Briarwood gives — the party are offered five errands and take any of the others.

Orphaned as-if: Redfang Castle · Halgrim Deepdelver · Ruins of Emberwillow · Stonesong Cave

## What this surfaces

- **Three of the four errands the party can still take are leaves.** Barrowfen,
  Old Elm Well and Basalt Tor carry no onward edge; exactly one of the five was
  load-bearing, and nothing in the fiction told them so.
- **Both destination nodes are unreachable, and were before the strike.** The
  strike does not create that — it removes the one edge that could ever have
  become a route to them, and takes Emberwillow down with it.
