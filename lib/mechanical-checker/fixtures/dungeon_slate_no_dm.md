# The Gloamfen Museum, after hours — keyed site

**Anchor:** the Gloamfen Museum of Natural History (node N-museum)
**Objective:** reach the Gemstone Wing strongroom before the papers burn (M7)
**Party:** 3 PCs, Level 5
**Scale:** one-session delve — 7 keyed areas, one level, 3 combats
**Signature technique:** Redundant level links — the grand stair, the goods
dumbwaiter and the records ladder each join the floors, so no stairhead is a chokepoint
**Dungeon mechanic:** the watch's escalating response

## The mechanic — the watch's escalating response

- **Trigger/Clock:** advances one step each time the party is seen, heard, or leaves
  a door open behind them.
- **Effect:** step 1 puts two more guards on the ground floor; step 2 closes the
  gallery shutters; step 3 sends 1d4 reinforcements per round from the nearest of the
  twelve fixed stations.
- **Tells:** whistle-pips down the service pipes, shutters running one gallery ahead
  of the party, the stations emptying in order.
- **Exploit:** the stations empty in a fixed order, so a party that draws the watch
  to one end of the building walks the other end unopposed.

## Keyed areas

- **M1 — Entrance hall.** The public way in, under the whale.
- **M2 — Ancient plants.** The west gallery, and the records ladder.
- **M3 — Rotunda.** The grand stair's head; the watch walks it.
- **M4 — Receiving dock.** The second approach, off the yard.
- **M5 — Basement stack.** Crates, the dumbwaiter, and no lighting.
- **M6 — Records room.** Dry, and the only lit room down here.
- **M7 — Gemstone Wing strongroom.** The papers, and the set piece.
