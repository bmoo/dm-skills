# A site

**Signature technique:** Route loops
**Dungeon mechanic:** two, unfortunately

## The generator phases

- **Trigger/Clock:** the dynamo cycles every 10 minutes.
- **Effect:** door-locks die in the off-phase.
- **Tells:** the dynamo's pitch falling.
- **Exploit:** cross in the dark phase.

## The alarm escalation

- **Trigger/Clock:** each tripped alarm.
- **Effect:** more guards arrive.
- **Tells:** klaxons.
- **Exploit:** cut the wire.
