Shinebright, the lead that carries his last known address.

The campaign's method handbook states no marking convention for lead typing, so
each edge is typed from its own entry's sentence and the reading is stated here.

## What this surfaces

- **This web is robust at the Oakhouse and the strike proves it.** Three inbound
  leads locate it and two of them are untouched; the College's inquiry is the
  loudest of the three and the least load-bearing.
- **Typing cost nothing here.** That is the point of doing it: it is free where
  the web has redundancy and it is the whole answer where it does not.
